The app's real interface, with sample goals.

Context

The week, one click away.

I wanted to see only this week's goals, without opening a full task manager or getting lost in long lists. It lives in the Mac menu bar: one click and it is there.

What it does

Few features, done well.

  • Weekly progress

    A ring shows the completion percentage and the count of finished goals from Monday to Sunday.

  • Days as chips

    Each day shows its progress (for example, 2/3) and turns green when complete.

  • Navigate weeks

    Go to the previous or next week, and jump back to today with one button.

  • Reminders

    Notifications Monday to Friday at noon and at 5 pm to review and close the day.

  • Quick to maintain

    Add, complete, delete and clear a day's completed goals. Everything is stored on the computer.
