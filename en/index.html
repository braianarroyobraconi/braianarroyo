What I do

I lead the UX team at NaranjaX, one of Argentina's largest fintechs. My focus is culture: a team close to the craft, always learning and working with the right tools.

Team and culture

Hiring, career growth, feedback and rituals. I build teams where senior and junior designers grow together, because product quality mirrors how the team works.

Process and quality

I shape how we design: quality standards, user research and decision-making with product and engineering. Craft doesn't go away when you lead. It becomes the team's bar.

AI in the design process

I explore how AI is reshaping design work, roles and collaboration with product, and I build internal tools so the team uses it day to day.
